What Makes an Online IQ Test Valid?

When assessing the quality of a free test, check these three factors:

  • Diverse Categories: It should evaluate multiple domains (spatial patterns, mathematical logic, and verbal relations) to avoid skewing the score.
  • Clear Calibration: Scores should be calibrated against standard bell curve distributions, where 100 represents the average.
  • Clear Terms: Transparent platforms explicitly state how your data is handled and state score calculations beforehand.
